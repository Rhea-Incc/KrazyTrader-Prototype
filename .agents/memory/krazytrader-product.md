name: KrazyTrader product context
description: Product audience, purpose, and phase boundary for the KrazyTrader mobile project.
---

KrazyTrader is a Kenyan mobility membership platform serving boda-boda riders and three-wheeler operators. Its intended network includes SACCOs, vehicle dealers and financing opportunities, insurance and emergency assistance, fuel, tyres, repair and maintenance partners, and other member benefits.

The user explicitly requested Phase 02 rider onboarding and SACCO membership, and chose to include a local demonstration financing workflow in the same phase. Financing must remain illustrative and must not connect to a real lender or credit service.

**Why:** The user clarified that financing should be demonstrated now even though the Phase 02 brief otherwise places it in a later phase.

**How to apply:** Finish only the requested scope and do not proceed to Phase 03 automatically. Treat demo accounts, applications, financing estimates and status controls as local prototype data, not live services or decisions.
