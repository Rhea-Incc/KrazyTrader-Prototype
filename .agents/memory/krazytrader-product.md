KrazyTrader is a Kenyan mobility membership platform serving boda-boda riders and three-wheeler operators. Its intended network includes SACCOs, vehicle dealers and financing opportunities, insurance and emergency assistance, fuel, tyres, repair and maintenance partners, and other member benefits.

The user requested Phase 01 as a foundation prototype and said not to proceed to Phase 02 automatically.

**Why:** This product context and phase boundary came from the user-provided brief and are not fully recoverable from the code alone.

**How to apply:** Keep future work focused on the phase the user explicitly requests. Demonstration data and routes are not live services or completed transactions.
